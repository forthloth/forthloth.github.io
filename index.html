013D tokenization · 2026

ZipTok3D ↗

High-fidelity 3D tokenization with compact token prefixes

ZipTok3D progressively packs object geometry into latent token prefixes. A shared decoder reconstructs detailed shapes from extremely short sequences, enabling efficient 3D generation.

02Embodied AI · 2026

Multi-view latent diffusion world model

Geometry-aware prediction for robot manipulation

Designed a multi-view world model in latent space. VGGT recovers camera geometry while geometry-aware attention fuses information across views, improving physical consistency in future-state prediction.
